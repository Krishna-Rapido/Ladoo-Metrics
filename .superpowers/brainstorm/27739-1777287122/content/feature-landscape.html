Ladoo Metrics: Feature Landscape

Which category excites you most? Pick the area(s) you want to invest in next.

A

Quick EDA & Data Profiling

For: Analysts on first upload

  • Auto column profiler (distributions, nulls, outliers, cardinality)
  • Correlation heatmap between all metrics
  • Distribution comparison (violin/box plots: test vs control)
  • Automatic data quality score + warnings
  • Quick "describe" summary like pandas-profiling in-browser

Impact: Every upload becomes 10x more informative before analysis even starts

B

Advanced Causal Inference Toolkit

For: Data Scientists running experiments

  • Propensity Score Matching (fix imperfect randomization)
  • CausalImpact (Bayesian time-series for pre/post without control)
  • Heterogeneous Treatment Effects (which segments benefit most?)
  • Synthetic Control Method (city-level experiments)
  • Regression Discontinuity Design

Impact: Move from "did it work?" to "why did it work and for whom?"

C

Self-Serve Dashboard Builder

For: PMs and Leadership

  • Drag-and-drop dashboard builder (tiles, layouts, filters)
  • KPI cards with targets, spark lines, and trend arrows
  • Cohort retention triangles (classic retention curves)
  • Scheduled email/Slack digest of dashboard snapshots
  • Shareable dashboard URLs (no login for read-only)

Impact: Leadership gets live metrics without asking analysts

D

Behavioral Data Science Lab

For: Senior DS doing deep captain analysis

  • Captain journey reconstruction (timeline of key events)
  • Survival analysis (time-to-churn Kaplan-Meier curves)
  • Auto-clustering (k-means/DBSCAN on behavioral features)
  • Uplift modeling (predict who responds to treatment)
  • Behavioral fingerprinting (engagement patterns by TOD/DOW)

Impact: Understand the "why" behind captain behavior, not just the "what"

E

Experiment Operations & Intelligence

For: The entire experiments org

  • Cross-experiment comparison table ("which experiments moved supply?")
  • Experiment power calculator (sample size before you start)
  • One-click readout (UUID in, formatted report out)
  • Guardrail metrics (auto-flag if experiment hurts safety metrics)
  • Experiment knowledge base (searchable history of all past experiments)

Impact: Build institutional memory, never repeat a failed experiment

F

Continuous Monitoring & Alerting

For: Ops teams and on-call analysts

  • Daily automated anomaly scan (captain supply drops, DAPR shifts)
  • Slack/email alerts with context (what changed, how much, since when)
  • Metric drift detection (gradual degradation that humans miss)
  • City x Service health scorecard (red/yellow/green)
  • "What you might have missed" digest panel

Impact: Catch problems before they become incidents
