Narrative: "The Bangalore incentive rollout caused a +14.2% increase in daily net rides (95% CI: [10.8%, 17.6%]). The posterior probability of a causal effect is 99.2%. The model achieved 2.1% MAPE on the pre-period, indicating excellent fit. The cumulative impact over 28 days is +4,230 additional net rides. The effect appears to be growing over time, suggesting captain behavior is still adapting."

Why this is powerful for Rapido: City-wide rollouts are common, and CausalImpact doesn't need a control group. The 3-panel layout (original vs counterfactual → pointwise → cumulative) tells a complete story that PMs can present to leadership without explanation.
